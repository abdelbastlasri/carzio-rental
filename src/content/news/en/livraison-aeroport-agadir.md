Delivery is one of the questions we get most before a booking. At Carzio it is free in Agadir and charged per trip elsewhere. Here is the full grid, with no surprise at key handover.

## Agadir Al Massira and the agency: free

Pickup at Agadir Al Massira airport and at our agency, MAG N2, Agadir Airport, is **included in the price**. No delivery fee is added.

## Marrakech and Casablanca: €80 per trip

For pickup in Marrakech or Casablanca, allow **€80 per trip**. The fee applies to delivery and, if the car is returned in that city, to the return trip. A rental delivered to and returned in Marrakech therefore includes €160 of delivery.

## Taghazout, Tamraght and Essaouira: on request

These destinations are served **on request**. The price is confirmed before booking, depending on the exact address and time, so you know the amount before you commit.

## What to include in the request

The form lets you choose the pickup location and the return location, then the dates and times. The more precise the information, the faster the confirmation.

## Fuel: the same level

The car is handed over at a fuel level and must be returned at the **same level**. Refuel near the airport before returning to avoid fuel charges.
