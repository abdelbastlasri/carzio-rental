Not every Carzio car has the same equipment. If GPS or a touchscreen matters for your trip, here are the models concerned, based on the fleet's actual equipment.

## Six models with GPS

- Peugeot 208
- Dacia Duster
- Kia Sportage
- VW T-Roc automatic
- Hyundai Tucson
- Volkswagen Touareg

## Eight models with a touchscreen

- Hyundai i10
- Peugeot 208
- Renault Clio 5
- Dacia Duster
- Kia Sportage
- VW T-Roc automatic
- Hyundai Tucson
- Volkswagen Touareg

The models that combine GPS and a touchscreen are therefore the 208, Duster, Sportage, T-Roc, Tucson and Touareg.

## How to check before booking

Each car card on the site lists the available equipment, including GPS and touchscreen. The information appears directly on the car card, before the form opens.

## What if your car has no GPS?

A smartphone is enough for navigation. Bring a mount and check that your data plan works in Morocco, or download an offline map before you leave.
