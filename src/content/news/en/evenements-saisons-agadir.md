Agadir works as a destination all year round, but demand for cars does not follow a straight line. Here are the periods worth knowing so you book at the right time.

## Agadir's climate: an advantage all year

Agadir has a mild climate: pleasant winters and warm summers tempered by the ocean. That is why the city stays busy almost all year. For car rental, it means one simple thing: the most requested models go first, especially automatics and 7-seaters.

## July and August: high season

This is the busiest period. European school holidays, the heat and the crowds on the Atlantic coast push demand up. Book **at least two to three weeks ahead**, and earlier if you need a specific model (Dacia Jogger 7-seater, automatic, SUV).

A few habits for this period:

- Choose an early-morning pickup at Agadir Al Massira airport.
- Carry water and avoid long drives during the hottest hours.
- Check the car's air conditioning on its card before confirming.

## December, January and the festive season

The holidays bring many visitors, especially those escaping the European winter. Demand is strong between Christmas and New Year, then gradually falls. If you travel with family, book the baby seat and booster with the car: they are free, but they must be planned for.

## Spring and autumn: the ideal window

From April to June and from September to November, the weather is very pleasant and pressure on the fleet is lower. This is the best time for a road trip: less traffic, mild temperatures and more choice. Prices and models are easier to secure, even a few days before departure.

## Ramadan

Ramadan dates change every year. During this period, local life runs at a different pace: some roads are quieter in the late afternoon, then busier after the fast is broken. Plan your journeys accordingly, especially if you cross a city at iftar time. Agencies and shops may also adjust their opening hours.

## School holidays and long weekends

Spring holidays, the autumn break and long weekends create short peaks. Even outside July and August, a single long weekend can absorb the most popular models. The rule stays the same: the firmer your dates, the earlier you should book.

## How to plan ahead

- **Fixed dates**: book as soon as your flight is confirmed.
- **Model**: state your real needs (passengers, luggage, automatic).
- **Options**: mention the child seat and second driver in your request, even though they are free.
- **Delivery**: pickup at Agadir Al Massira airport and at the agency is free; for Marrakech and Casablanca, allow €80 per trip.
- **Cancellation**: free up to 24 hours before pickup, so you can book early without risk.
