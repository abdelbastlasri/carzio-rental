The advertised price does not tell the whole story. Here is what a Carzio rental includes, what remains your responsibility, and how to avoid surprises when you pay.

## What the price includes

The daily rate shown on each car card includes:

- **Standard insurance**, covering the vehicle, glass, tyres and mirrors.
- **Unlimited mileage**.
- The **second driver**, free.
- The **baby seat and booster**, free on request.
- **Delivery to Agadir Al Massira airport** and to our MAG N2, Agadir Airport agency.
- **Free cancellation** up to 24 hours before pickup.

The minimum rental is 72 hours, billed in 24-hour blocks. Any started block counts as a full day: that is the most important rule to build into your budget.

## The deposit: 0 MAD

At Carzio there is **no deposit and no card hold**. That is a real budget advantage: your card limit stays available for the whole trip. The card is used to pay for the rental at key handover.

## Full insurance, optional

Standard insurance includes an excess that remains your responsibility in the event of covered damage. If you would rather remove that risk, **Full Risk insurance** reduces the excess to 0 MAD. It is available from 5 rental days, and its price is already included in the total shown before you confirm. For a one-week trip, compare that supplement with the potential cost of damage: it is often the best-value item in the budget.

## Delivery by destination

- **Agadir Al Massira and the agency**: free.
- **Marrakech-Menara and Casablanca Mohammed V**: €80 per trip, on pickup and, if the car is returned there, on return.
- **Taghazout, Tamraght and Essaouira**: price on request, confirmed before booking.

For on-request destinations, ask for the price on WhatsApp before confirming: then you know exactly what you pay.

## Fuel

The rule is simple: **return the car at the same fuel level as at pickup**. Fill up just before returning, keep the receipt and avoid handing the car back almost empty. This avoids refuelling charges, which are often higher than the fuel itself.

## Tolls and parking

Moroccan motorways are tolled, especially on the Agadir–Marrakech axis. Keep some cash or an accepted bank card for the toll booth. In town, parking is often paid or looked after by an attendant: a few dirhams are enough, but it helps to have them with you.

## Avoidable costs

- **Unnecessary upgrades**: an SUV is not required on the paved roads around Agadir. A Sandero or Logan is enough for a couple or a small family.
- **Late returns**: a started 24-hour block is charged. Tell us in advance if your flight is delayed.
- **Skipping the refuel**: fill up before returning the car.
- **Fines**: respect speed limits and parking rules. A fine remains the driver's responsibility.

## Display prices in your currency

The site shows prices in **Moroccan dirham (MAD)** by default and can convert them to euros, US dollars or pounds sterling using fixed rates. The contract stays the same: the currency changes the display, not the rental conditions.

## In short

A rental budget in Agadir is made of the daily rate, fuel, any tolls and, if you choose it, Full Risk insurance. With a 0 MAD deposit, a free second driver and free child seats, most of the essentials are already in the price.
