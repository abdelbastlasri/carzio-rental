Travelling with children takes a little more planning: child seats, luggage, long drives. Here is how Carzio keeps family rentals simple.

## Choose the right car

For 2 adults + 2 children, a saloon such as the **Dacia Logan** or **Renault Clio 5** is enough. For a larger group or a lot of luggage, choose the **Dacia Jogger 7-seater** (7 seats, generous boot) or the **Dacia Duster**. The Jogger is the best fit for families travelling with a pushchair and a cabin bag per person.

## Child seats are free

At Carzio, the **baby seat** and **booster seat** are free on request. Tell us the number and age of your children in the special requests when you book, and we will fit the right seat for each child.

## Air conditioning matters

In July and August, or during any hot spell, air conditioning is not a luxury: it keeps the children comfortable and helps the driver stay alert. All our cars are air-conditioned. Check the car card for the model year and equipment.

## Practical tips for the drive

- **Leave early in the morning**: motorways are quieter and the children travel while it is still cool.
- **Take water and snacks**: restaurants can be sparse outside towns.
- **Plan a break every two hours**: service areas on the N8 are practical for a short stop.
- **Never leave a child alone in the car**, not even for a minute or in the shade: temperatures rise very quickly.
- **Book early**: the Jogger and the SUVs go first in high season.

## What to bring for the trip

- Licence (European, Moroccan or international), passport or ID card, and the payment card used for the booking. The deposit is 0 MAD: no card hold.
- Sunglasses, sun cream and an offline map if your mobile plan does not cover Morocco.
- A simple list of planned stops so nobody is caught out.
