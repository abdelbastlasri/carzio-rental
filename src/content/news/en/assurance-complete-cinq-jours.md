At Carzio, standard insurance is included in the price. Full insurance is an option that removes the excess, available from 5 billed days. Here is how the two work together, without the jargon.

## Standard insurance is included

It covers the vehicle and includes the glass, tyres and mirrors. In case of covered damage, an excess remains your responsibility: it is the maximum you pay for the repairs. The amount appears in the contract signed at key handover.

## Full insurance, from 5 billed days

The full option brings the excess down to **0 MAD**: in case of covered damage, nothing remains your responsibility. It is offered only from **5 billed days**. Rentals are billed in 24-hour blocks and any started block counts as a full day: a rental of 4 days and 10 hours counts as 5 days, and the option becomes available.

The surcharge depends on the vehicle's daily rate (10 € per day on the lowest rates, 15 € per day on higher ones) and is always shown before confirmation.

## A worked example

For 5 days at €30 per day, full insurance costs €10 per day, or **€50 for the rental**. The excess drops to 0 MAD. Over 7 days at €45 per day, the surcharge is €15 per day, or **€105**: the excess disappears.

## It is never ticked by default

The option is never pre-selected. If you go below 5 billed days, it is unticked automatically and disappears from the total. You choose with full knowledge.
