At Carzio, standard insurance is included in the price. Full insurance is an option that removes the excess, available from 5 billed days. Here is how the two work together, without the jargon.

## Standard insurance is included

It covers the vehicle, including glass, tyres and mirrors. If the car is damaged, an excess remains your responsibility: it is the maximum you pay for covered repairs.

## Two excess amounts by daily rate

- **Car at €39 per day or less:** a **5,000 MAD** excess.
- **Car at €40 per day or more:** an **8,000 MAD** excess.

The daily rate is the price shown on the car card, not the total divided by the number of days.

## Full insurance from 5 billed days

The full option brings the excess down to **0 MAD**. It is offered only from **5 billed days**:

- **+€10 per day** for cars at €39 per day or less;
- **+€15 per day** for cars at €40 per day or more.

Rentals are billed in 24-hour blocks. Any started block counts as a full day: a 4-day, 10-hour rental counts as 5 billed days, so the option becomes available.

## A concrete example

For a car at €30 per day over 5 days, full insurance costs €10 per day, or **€50 for the rental**. The 5,000 MAD excess drops to 0 MAD.

## It is never pre-selected

The option is never ticked by default. If you go below 5 billed days, it is automatically unticked and removed from the total, so you always choose knowingly.
