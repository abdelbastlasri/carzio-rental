The deposit is often the stressful part of a rental. At Carzio there is none: **0 MAD deposit**, no hold on your card, and payment at key handover. Here is how it works in practice.

## No deposit, no hold

No amount is charged or reserved on your card when you book. Debit cards are accepted, as well as credit cards, so your spending limit stays available during the trip.

## Payment at key handover

Payment is made when the car is handed over, by bank card. The amount is the rental price, with standard insurance included.

## Minimum duration and billing

The minimum rental is **72 hours**, billed in 24-hour blocks. Any started block counts as a full day.

## Free cancellation up to 24 h before

You can cancel free of charge up to **24 hours before pickup**. Confirmation is sent by email to the client, with a copy to contact@carzio.ma.

## Documents to bring

The main driver must present:

- a valid driving licence held for at least 4 years;
- an ID card or passport;
- a bank card for payment.

The driver must be **at least 23 years old**. A second driver can be added free of charge, under the same conditions. Baby seats and boosters are also free, on request.
