Agadir, Essaouira and Marrakech are ideal to explore by car. Distances are clear, the roads are paved, and each city has its own atmosphere. Here is a realistic route, with the costs and driving times.

## Agadir: the base

Agadir is compact, flat and easy. The **Corniche** is perfect to walk, run or cycle. The **Souk El Had** is the country's largest market: opening hours vary by day, and taxis are frequent around it. The **Oufella valley** offers a view of the bay and the Kasbah. In Agadir itself, the car is mainly useful for nearby beaches (Taghazout, Tamraght).

## Essaouira: 3 hours from Agadir

Essaouira is the most relaxing option: fortified medina, beaches, fishing port and terrace seafood restaurants. Allow about **3 hours** along the coast (N1). Stop at Taghazout or Tamraght on the way if you love the coast.

## Marrakech: 6 hours from Agadir

Marrakech demands the most driving. The N8 crosses quiet plateaus; allow about **6 hours**. The historic centre is pedestrian around Jemaa el Fna: leave the car at the riad or in a dedicated car park, never double-parked. The **marrakech souks** and the gardens (Majorelle, Yves Saint Laurent) are best visited in the morning.

## Three tips to get the loop right

- **Leave early**: collecting at 8 am maximises your day in Marrakech or Essaouira.
- **Favour weekdays**: in July–August and school holidays, roads and car parks fill quickly.
- **Book a hotel with parking**: in Marrakech, avoid unauthorised stops in the historic centre.

## What to see first

- **Agadir**: Corniche, Souk El Had, Kasbah Oufella, Agadir and Aït Melloul beaches.
- **Essaouira**: medina, port, Portuguese fortifications, surf beach.
- **Marrakech**: Jemaa el Fna, souks, Majorelle and YSL gardens, Bahia palace.
- **Tamraght/Taghazout**: surf, beach restaurants, fishing villages.
