Safety is more about reflexes than about memorised rules. Here is what changes most on Moroccan roads, and what to keep in mind day to day.

## Seatbelts and child seats

Seatbelts are mandatory in front, for driver and passenger. Children sit in the back in a seat adapted to their size. At Carzio, baby and booster seats are **free** on request: tell us your child's age in special requests when booking.

## Speed limits

The general idea: roughly **60 within built-up areas, 100 on secondary roads, 120 on motorways**. When in doubt, the sign wins. At night, reduce further: many roads have no lighting and animals are sometimes unpredictable.

## Checks

Speed and police checks are frequent. Always carry your licence, the vehicle registration and the rental contract. Never negotiate a check: stay calm, present your documents, and move on.

## In case of an incident or accident

- Secure the people first: hazard lights on, park safely, then **call 190** (police) if necessary.
- **Do not move the vehicles** before the authorities arrive, unless there is a clear danger.
- Call Carzio on +212 680 318003: we guide you step by step.
- If anyone is injured, see a doctor and keep the accident documents.

## Parking

- Prefer guarded car parks in busy areas.
- Never double-park: it is the quickest way to your car being towed.
- Never leave valuables visible inside.

## Mountain roads and rain

Winding roads demand more distance and more anticipation. In rain, wet surfaces become slippery: reduce speed, increase distance and keep your lights on.

## The 5 reflexes to remember

1. Slow down at every built-up area entry, before the sign.
2. A generous following distance: at least 2 seconds.
3. No handheld phone, ever — even at traffic lights.
4. Documents at hand for every check.
5. One eye on the road, one eye on signage.
