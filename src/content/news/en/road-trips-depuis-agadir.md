Agadir is an ideal base for driving in southern Morocco: sea, mountains, souks and imperial cities. Here are three realistic rental-car itineraries, with distances and practical tips.

## 1. The coast loop: Agadir – Taghazout – Tamraght – Essaouira

**Distance**: about 480 km round trip · **Duration**: 5–6 hours on the road over 2 days · **Cost**: free pickup in Agadir; negotiated rate on arrival and return in Essaouira (on request).

Day 1: Agadir → Taghazout (20 minutes) → Tamraght, lunch on a terrace, surf if you like the water. Day 2: early start to Essaouira along the N1 (about 3 hours). Stop in Tamraght or Sidi Kaouki. Come back the next day.

## 2. Marrakech in a weekend

**Distance**: about 1,000 km round trip · **Duration**: about 6 hours each way · **Cost**: delivery in Marrakech **€80** per trip (outbound or return).

Fill up, leave at 7 am from Agadir, arrive around 1 pm in Marrakech. Jemaa el Fna by evening, souks the next morning, Majorelle gardens for the photo. One night there, collection the next day.

## 3. Paradise Valley in a day

**Distance**: about 150 km round trip · **Duration**: 3–4 hours on the road · **Access**: paved road to the last village, then a light track.

Paradise Valley is the perfect escape to the green: small Berber villages, clear pools, a few hiker cafés. Ideal in spring and autumn. In summer, leave early to avoid the heat. Take plenty of water and check road conditions before going off route.

## Practical tips for these routes

- **Driving times**: Moroccan roads are pleasant if you take your time; allow extra, especially in the mountains.
- **Fuel**: fill up in town or on the N8 — stations are rarer on secondary roads.
- **Breaks**: every 2 hours, roughly — your passengers stay fresh and the road is safer.
- **Night driving**: avoid it if you don't know the route; return to Agadir before dark whenever possible.
- **The car**: a Stepway or Logan is perfect for these loops; an SUV is not required on standard paved roads.

## Another loop: Agadir – Casablanca

Casablanca is about **6 hours** from Agadir. Delivery possible in Casablanca Mohammed V: **€80** per trip. Ideal for arriving and departing from Casablanca rather than Marrakech.
