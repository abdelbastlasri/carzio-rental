Insurance is often the least-read part of a rental agreement. Yet it decides what you pay if the car is damaged. At Carzio there are two options: standard insurance, included, and full insurance, optional from 5 days. Here is how they work, without the jargon.

## What standard insurance covers

Standard insurance is **included in the rental price**. It covers the vehicle, including glass, tyres and mirrors. If damage occurs, an excess remains your responsibility: it is the maximum amount you may have to pay for the damage concerned.

The excess depends on the car's daily rate, not on its size or category.

## Two excess levels based on the daily rate

- **Car at €39 per day or less:** **5,000 MAD** excess.
- **Car at €40 per day or more:** **8,000 MAD** excess.

The daily rate is the one shown on the vehicle page when you book. If the price changes during the rental, the excess stated in the agreement signed at pickup still applies.

## What full insurance adds

Full insurance reduces the excess to **0 MAD**. For covered damage, nothing is left for you to pay. It is offered as an option, with a daily supplement:

- **+€10 per day** for a car at €39 per day or less;
- **+€15 per day** for a car at €40 per day or more.

The option is **never pre-ticked**. You choose it yourself in the booking form and see the new total immediately.

## When is full insurance available?

Full insurance is available **from 5 billed days**. Below that threshold, the option is not available and cannot be added.

Duration is calculated in 24-hour blocks, and any started day is charged. A rental of 4 days and 10 hours is therefore billed as 5 days and qualifies; a rental of exactly 4 days (96 hours) remains at 4 days. When in doubt, the form shows the number of billed days before you confirm.

If you later shorten the rental below 5 days, full insurance is automatically unticked and the total recalculated. You never pay for an option that does not apply to your rental.

## Three worked examples

**Example 1: 5 days, car at €30 per day.**
Rental: 5 × €30 = €150. Full insurance: 5 × €10 = €50. Total: **€200**, 0 MAD excess.

**Example 2: 7 days, car at €45 per day.**
Rental: 7 × €45 = €315. Full insurance: 7 × €15 = €105. Total: **€420**, 0 MAD excess.

**Example 3: 4 days, car at €35 per day.**
Full insurance is not available because the rental is shorter than 5 days. Standard cover applies, with a 5,000 MAD excess for damage.

## How to choose

Full insurance costs a few euros per day but removes the excess. It is especially useful on long trips, on unfamiliar roads or when you often park in busy urban areas.

Standard insurance suits you if you are comfortable with an excess and your card or travel insurance already covers part of the risk. Check your card terms carefully: not all cards cover rentals in Morocco, and limits vary.

Either way, compare the option cost with the excess amount. For a 5-day rental at €30 per day, full insurance costs €50 and reduces a 5,000 MAD excess to zero. The maths is quick.

In a serious emergency, call **190**. For any vehicle incident, contact us first: we will tell you what to do under your cover.

## What insurance does not replace

Insurance covers vehicle damage under the selected option. It does not replace fuel, fines, parking charges or the consequences of driving outside the agreement. The terms specifically set fees for a lost key (€150) or lost vehicle documents (€500).

Those amounts are not insurance excesses: they are administrative fees set out in the agreement. They apply whichever option you choose.

## In short

- Standard insurance is included, with a 5,000 MAD excess (car at €39/day or less) or 8,000 MAD (car at €40/day or more).
- Full insurance reduces the excess to 0 MAD, for +€10/day or +€15/day depending on the rate.
- It is available from 5 rental days, never pre-ticked, and unticked automatically if the duration drops below 5 days.
- If damage occurs, contact us before any repair.
