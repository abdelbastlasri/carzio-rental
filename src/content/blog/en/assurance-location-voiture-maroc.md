At Carzio, standard insurance is included in the price of every rental. Full insurance is an optional add-on, offered from 5 billed days. Here is how the two work together, without the jargon.

## Standard insurance is included

It covers the vehicle and includes the glass, tyres and mirrors. If the car is damaged, an excess remains your responsibility: it is the maximum amount you pay for the covered repairs. Its amount is stated in the contract you sign at key handover.

## Full insurance, from 5 days

Full insurance reduces the excess to **0 MAD**: in case of covered damage, nothing remains your responsibility. It is offered only from **5 billed days**. Rentals are billed in 24-hour blocks and any started block counts as a full day: a rental of 4 days and 10 hours counts as 5 days, and the option becomes available.

The surcharge depends on the vehicle's daily rate (10 € per day on the lowest rates, 15 € per day on higher ones) and is always shown before confirmation.

## How to choose

Standard insurance is enough for most rentals: it covers the vehicle and its glass, tyres and mirrors. Full insurance is worth it if you drive a lot, on unfamiliar roads, or park often in busy areas: it removes all financial responsibility in case of covered damage.

Your bank card may already cover part of the risk of renting in Morocco: check your limits and exclusions before deciding. Some cards do not cover rentals in Morocco, or only partly.

## What is not covered

Intentional damage, use outside Morocco, breach of the contract (unauthorised use or destination) and interior damage are not covered, whichever plan you choose. The contract handed over at key handover sets out the exclusions and the responsibilities of each party.

## Practical tips

- Check the car at key handover and note every anomaly on the contract.
- Everything is recorded at handover: what is not written at pickup cannot be disputed at return.
- Avoid night driving outside urban areas if you don't know the road.
