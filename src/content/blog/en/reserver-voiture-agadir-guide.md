Renting a car in Agadir can be straightforward when you know the rules. At Carzio, you send a request online, receive confirmation by email and pay when the keys are handed over. This guide walks through each step, the documents to bring and what to check before you drive away.

## How far in advance should you book?

One to two weeks ahead is comfortable, especially in July and August. Last-minute requests are still possible: we check real availability and reply within 24 hours. A booking is only confirmed after that exchange, never automatically.

## Step 1: choose your dates and pickup point

Enter your pickup date and time, then your return date and time. You can collect the car at Agadir Al Massira airport, at our Agadir agency or in another city we serve.

The minimum rental is **3 days, or 72 hours**. Rental is billed in 24-hour blocks: any started day is charged, even if you return the car earlier that day. A morning return after three nights therefore counts as three days.

## Step 2: send your request

Pick a model from the fleet and submit the form. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp at [+212 680 318003](https://wa.me/212680318003) if you are unsure which car fits your trip.

No payment is taken online and no card is stored at this stage. The request does not block the vehicle yet: it lets us check availability for your dates.

## Step 3: receive confirmation

We check the calendar and send confirmation by email. A copy goes to contact@carzio.ma. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder or message us on WhatsApp.

The confirmation lists the model, dates, pickup point, total price and any delivery fee. Check those details before replying: correcting something now avoids surprises on the day.

## Step 4: pay at key handover

Payment is made when the car is handed over, by card, **including debit cards**. There is **no deposit, no security hold and no card pre-authorisation**. You pay the confirmed price, with no extra amount blocked on your account.

## Documents to bring

The main driver must present:

- a valid driving licence held for at least 4 years;
- an ID card or passport;
- a bank card for payment.

The driver must be **at least 23 years old**. These conditions apply to every car in the fleet, with no young-driver surcharge. A second driver can be added free of charge: they must meet the same conditions and show their licence at pickup. See our guide to [age, licence and drivers](/blog/age-permis-conducteurs-location-maroc) for details.

## Delivery and return: what to plan for

Delivery is free at Agadir Al Massira airport and at our agency, MAG N2, Agadir Airport. For Marrakech and Casablanca, allow **€80 per trip**: the fee applies to delivery and, if the car is returned there, to the return trip.

Taghazout, Tamraght and Essaouira are served **on request**. The price is confirmed before booking, depending on the exact address and time.

Leave some margin around your flight. On arrival, the vehicle check and contract signing take a few minutes. On return, refuel to the same level as at pickup to avoid fuel charges.

## Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Cancellation is **free up to 24 hours before pickup**. Date or vehicle changes are possible subject to availability, at no extra cost. If your flight is delayed, tell us on WhatsApp as early as possible so we can arrange the handover.

## What the price includes

Every rental includes:

- standard insurance, with glass, tyres and mirrors covered;
- unlimited mileage;
- a second driver;
- a baby seat or booster, on request;
- free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.

Full insurance, which reduces the excess to 0 MAD, is available from 5 rental days. The amounts are explained in our guide to [standard versus full insurance](/blog/assurance-location-voiture-maroc).

## What to check at key handover

Take a few minutes to walk around the car with our team:

1. Note the fuel level and starting mileage.
2. Photograph any existing scratches or dents.
3. Check that the registration document and insurance certificate are in the car.
4. Test the lights, wipers and air conditioning.
5. Ask where the spare wheel and tools are kept.

These checks are quick and protect both sides. Keep the photos until the end of the rental.

## In short

To book with confidence in Agadir: choose dates of at least 3 days, send the request, wait for confirmation, then pay at key handover. Bring a licence held for 4 years, an ID document and a bank card. If anything is unclear, ask before booking: our team replies on WhatsApp.
