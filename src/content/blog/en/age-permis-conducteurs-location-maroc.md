Before booking a rental car in Morocco, two conditions always come up: the driver's age and how long they have held a licence. At Carzio they are simple and identical for every vehicle. Here is what to know before sending your request.

## The two requirements

To drive a Carzio car, the main driver must:

- be **at least 23 years old**;
- have held a driving licence for **at least 4 years**.

These rules apply to **every vehicle in the fleet**, from city cars to SUVs. There is no category for younger drivers and no young-driver surcharge.

Age and licence history are checked at pickup against the original licence. A booking may be refused if the driver does not meet these conditions, even if the online request was accepted.

## Why must the licence be 4 years old?

Licence history is separate from age. A 25-year-old who has just passed their test does not qualify, while a 23-year-old who got their licence at 19 does.

A probationary licence is therefore not accepted, whatever the driver's age. If you have held your licence for less than 4 years, wait until the anniversary date before booking or contact us to check your situation.

## The second driver is free

You can add a second driver **at no extra cost**. They must meet the same conditions: at least 23 years old and a licence held for 4 years.

Give their name when booking and show their licence at pickup. The agreement must list both drivers so that each one is properly declared.

## Which documents to bring?

At pickup, bring:

- the main driver's **original driving licence**;
- an **ID card or passport**;
- a **bank card** for payment; debit cards are accepted.

No deposit or card hold is required. Payment is made at key handover, at the confirmed price.

## Are foreign licences accepted?

Yes. A legible foreign licence is accepted. Depending on its language and format, an **international driving permit** or a **translation** may be required. If in doubt, send a photo of your licence on WhatsApp before booking: we will check quickly.

The licence must be valid for the whole rental. An expired or illegible licence prevents the car from being handed over.

## What about children?

**Baby seats and boosters are free.** State your child's age and weight in the special requests when booking. We fit the right seat before handing over the keys.

We recommend planning the right seat when booking rather than improvising on arrival.

## Mistakes to avoid before booking

1. **Booking in the name of a driver who will not qualify.** The agreement is made in the main driver's name.
2. **Forgetting the second driver.** Add them to the request, even if they will drive only a little.
3. **Showing a probationary licence.** It will not be accepted, even with a supervising driver.
4. **Arriving without a bank card.** Payment is made at key handover, by card.
5. **Sending a blurry licence photo.** A clear copy avoids a hold-up on the day.

## In short

- **Minimum age 23** and **licence held for 4 years** for every vehicle.
- **No young-driver surcharge.**
- **Free second driver**, under the same conditions.
- **Foreign licences accepted**, with an international permit or translation if needed.
- **Probationary licences not accepted.**
- **Free baby seats and boosters**, on request.
